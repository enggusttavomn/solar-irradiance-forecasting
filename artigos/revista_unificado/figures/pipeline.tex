\caption{End-to-end matched forecasting design. (A) Independent NSRDB archives
are audited and converted into four direct-output tasks; learned models

(Algorithm~\ref{alg:matched-training} details the focal protocol); persistence,
seasonal na\"ive, and climatology require no training. (C) Direct test
forecasts from Algorithms~\ref{alg:timesnet-direct} and
\ref{alg:dilated-direct} receive common physical post-processing before
location-first aggregation, paired comparison, and benchmark ranking;
K\"oppen--Geiger and NASA POWER fields enter only after errors are fixed.}
