\section{Complete Reference-Model Benchmark}
This supplement compares TimesNet and DilatedRNN with all evaluated reference
models at each cumulative forecast horizon.
Table~\ref{tab:desempenho-macro-auditavel} reports the complete macro-MAE
comparison, and Figure~\ref{fig:supp_ranking} shows how model rankings vary
across horizons. Figure~\ref{fig:supp_seeds} summarizes variability across
random seeds for all learned models.
