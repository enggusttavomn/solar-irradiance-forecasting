\section{Complete Reference-Model Benchmark}
The main manuscript focuses on TimesNet and DilatedRNN. This section retains
all evaluated models and cumulative checkpoints, including competitors that
outperform the focal architectures. No model is excluded based on its score.
Table~\ref{tab:desempenho-macro-auditavel} reports the complete cumulative
macro-MAE comparison, and Figure~\ref{fig:supp_ranking} displays its ranking
trajectories. Figure~\ref{fig:supp_seeds} compares seed-level variability
across all learned models.
