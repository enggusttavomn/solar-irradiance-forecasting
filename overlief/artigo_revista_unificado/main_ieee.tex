\title{Multi-Resolution Global Horizontal Irradiance Forecasting at Electric-Vehicle Manufacturing Locations: TimesNet and Dilated Recurrent Networks}
\author{Gustavo de Melo Nascimento, Thales Wulfert Cabral, and Gustavo Fraidenraich}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This study presents a matched multi-resolution evaluation of global horizontal
irradiance (GHI) forecasting at ten electric-vehicle manufacturing locations.
TimesNet and DilatedRNN share chronological splits, origins, inputs,
post-processing, and location-first aggregation within each task. At the
terminal cumulative horizons, their respective macro-MAEs were 52.21 and
52.96~W/m$^2$ at 72 hours, 44.08 and 43.85~W/m$^2$ at 30 days, 13.93
and 13.01~W/m$^2$ at one month, and 14.51 and 14.37~W/m$^2$ at six
months. TimesNet led the hourly macro-average at every checkpoint and the
daily comparison through 14 days; DilatedRNN led at 30 days and in the
principal one-month task. Location-level wins sometimes reversed the aggregate
ranking, so neither architecture was universally superior. Daily and monthly
learned models used five seeds, whereas the hourly extension used one. Claims
remain descriptive because multi-step targets overlap and meteorology is used
only as post-hoc context.
\end{abstract}
