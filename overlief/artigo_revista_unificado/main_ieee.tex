\begin{abstract}
This journal extension consolidates hourly TimesNet and monthly DilatedRNN
studies of global horizontal irradiance forecasting at ten locations associated
with electric-vehicle factories. It also defines the daily and matched-horizon
experiments needed for a fair multi-resolution comparison. Existing errors are
not compared across temporal resolutions because their targets and protocols
