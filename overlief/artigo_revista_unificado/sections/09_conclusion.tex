\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This journal extension establishes a common framework for evaluating TimesNet
and DilatedRNN across hourly, daily, and longer planning resolutions at ten
electric-vehicle manufacturing locations. The existing source studies provide
promising but resolution-specific evidence: TimesNet is competitive in direct
hourly forecasting, while DilatedRNN is competitive for one-month-ahead
forecasting from short monthly series.
