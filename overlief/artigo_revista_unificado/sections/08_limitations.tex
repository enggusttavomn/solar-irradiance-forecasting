\section{Threats to Validity and Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

The NSRDB values are satellite-derived, model-based estimates and not sensors
installed at the factories. The factory sample is geographic and does not
represent industrial energy consumption or photovoltaic output. Monthly
training series contain only four complete pre-test target years, whereas
overlapping hourly forecast windows produce dependent errors. These properties
limit conventional significance tests that assume independent samples.

Architecture comparisons may also be confounded by parameter count, context
length, and optimization budget. The matched experiments will therefore report
capacity, search space, selected configuration, random seeds, and forecast
strategy. Generalization beyond the ten locations and the 2019--2024 period
requires a prospective evaluation on new sites or years.
