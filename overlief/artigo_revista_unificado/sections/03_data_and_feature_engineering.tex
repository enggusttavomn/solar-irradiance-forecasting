\section{Data and Feature Engineering}
\label{sec:data}

The study uses the GOES Aggregated Physical Solar Model v4 product from the
National Solar Radiation Database. The common geographic sample comprises ten
locations associated with electric-vehicle factories in Brazil, Mexico, and
the United States. Coordinates select the corresponding NSRDB grid cells and
are not interpreted as factory-level irradiance measurements.

Table~\ref{tab:variables} separates measured or retrieved fields from derived
attributes. This distinction is necessary because the hourly and monthly
studies currently use different representations. The unified experiments will
fit every transformation on pre-test data only and will document the exact
input available at each forecast origin.

Before modeling, each series is audited for ordering, duplicate timestamps,
coverage, missing values, non-finite values, and physically invalid negative
GHI. Aggregation is performed only after this audit. Scaling parameters,
quantization boundaries when used, and other learned transformations are
estimated from training data and then reused unchanged in validation and test.
This ordering prevents information from future periods from influencing model
development.
