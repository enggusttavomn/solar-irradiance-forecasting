\title{Multi-Resolution Global Horizontal Irradiance Forecasting at Electric-Vehicle Manufacturing Locations: TimesNet and Dilated Recurrent Networks}

\begin{abstract}
This journal extension investigates global horizontal irradiance forecasting at
ten locations associated with electric-vehicle factories across complementary
temporal resolutions. It consolidates an hourly TimesNet study and a monthly
DilatedRNN study under a common methodological description and defines the
additional daily and matched-horizon experiments required for a fair
cross-model comparison. The hourly study uses 336 hours of context to predict
the following 72 hours, while the monthly study predicts one month ahead from
short multiyear series. Because their current targets, horizons, sample sizes,
and primary metrics differ, their published errors are reported as prior
evidence rather than compared directly. The completed extension will evaluate
TimesNet and DilatedRNN under identical chronological splits, forecast origins,
post-processing rules, and location-level aggregation at every shared temporal
resolution. \DraftNote{Replace this final sentence with consolidated numerical

\end{abstract}
